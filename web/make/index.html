    Where to find them

    1. In Finder, open the Go menu and choose Go to Folder…
    2. Type ~/Library/Messages and press Return.
    3. Drag chat.db and chat.db-wal from that folder onto the box above.

    Prefer the Choose files button? In the window that opens, press ⌘ ⇧ G, type the same folder, and select both files.

    Why two files: chat.db-wal holds your newest messages. Without it the last few days can be missing.

    Show names instead of phone numbers (optional)

    A Mac keeps contact names in a separate place. Go to ~/Library/Application Support/AddressBook and add every file whose name ends in .abcddb (look inside the Sources folders too), together with the two files above or afterwards.

    You can also skip this: step 3 lets you type the names yourself.

    How to get a backup this page can read

    1. Connect the iPhone to a computer and back it up there: with Finder on a Mac, with Apple Devices (or iTunes) on Windows. Leave Encrypt local backup unticked.
    2. Open the folder that holds your backups: Mac ~/Library/Application Support/MobileSync/Backup Windows %USERPROFILE%\Apple\MobileSync\Backup Windows, older iTunes %APPDATA%\Apple Computer\MobileSync\Backup
    3. Inside is a folder with a long name made of letters and numbers. Drop it on the box above, or pick it with Choose backup folder.

    Your browser may ask whether to “upload” the folder. That is its word for letting this page read the files. They stay on your computer.

    Quicker for a big backup: use Choose the two files and pick 3d/3d0d7e5f…3d28 (your messages) and 31/31bb7ba8…e442 (contact names, optional) from inside that folder.

    When you are done, delete the backup or switch its encryption back on.

    Have a list of messages from the iPhone Shortcut or an export app? Paste it here, or choose the file. Experimental

    What can I paste?

    Each message needs a date and who sent it; the text is optional. Any of these shapes works:

    • Two lists (what the Shortcut makes): a line #dates, one date per line, then a line #senders and one name per line. An empty name means you sent it.
    • One message per line: {"date": "…", "sender": "…", "text": "…"}
    • A JSON list of the same items.
    • A spreadsheet file (CSV or TSV) with a header row naming the date, sender and text columns.

    Tapbacks and photos are not part of an export, so those parts of the recap stay empty.
